About the Platform

The IIPMP facilitates comprehensive monitoring of major infrastructure projects across the nation, ensuring better coordination, risk mitigation, and efficient utilization of public funds.

Real-Time Monitoring

Track physical and financial progress of projects in real-time. Identify bottlenecks instantly through unified dashboards.

Smart Risk Detection

Automated flagging of projects facing potential delays or cost overruns to enable proactive interventions and mitigation.

Data-Driven Insights

Comprehensive analytics across ministries, states, and sectors to support evidence-based policy making and resource allocation.

How It Works

Data Collection

Agencies input project milestones and expenditure data regularly.

Analysis

System correlates physical progress against financial spend.

Monitoring

Dashboards highlight deviations, risks, and performance metrics.

Reporting

Generates actionable insights and alerts for decision-makers.
